Many American-option lattices require implied volatility (IV), which is recovered from an observed option price. We present a pipeline that fits IV offline and simulates option markets without contemporaneous quotes. A Jump Hidden Markov Model generates stock paths. A neural surface sets contract variance targets from calendar maturity and moneyness. Square-root factors evolve around those targets, and a lattice prices American options and Greeks. The construction is inspired by Heston's variance process, but it is not a standard Heston model because each contract has its own factor and the stock is generated separately. On 234{,}549 observations from 31 tickers and fifteen dates, a global neural surface reduced in-sample IV root mean squared error from 12.48 to 11.47 volatility percentage points; sector networks reached 10.24 points. A single-date split had train and test errors of 10.21 and 10.89 points. On a separate two-day holdout, earnings inputs reduced test error from 13.03 to 11.98 points, although the design did not isolate a causal earnings effect. Fitted Goldman Sachs and Eli Lilly illustrations used 1{,}000 paths, 31 calendar days, and 22 trading transitions. Dynamic IV determined interim marks and Greeks, while held-to-expiration profit and loss depended only on the JumpHMM terminal stock price and payoff. The implementation is open source.
