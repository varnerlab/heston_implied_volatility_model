\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We developed a modular pipeline that combined JumpHMM stock paths, fitted IV surfaces, contract-specific square-root factors, and American-option lattices. Neural surfaces reduced in-sample IV error, and earnings inputs improved one temporal holdout. These results support the representation within the tested data window; they do not establish broad temporal generalization.

The paired dynamic ablation showed that the choice of IV dynamics materially changed interim marks and liquidation P\&L on identical stock paths, while terminal payoffs remained identical. Negative return coupling changed put and call downside exposure in different directions. Numerical refinement left the sampled marks nearly unchanged, but the strike audit identified residual GS inconsistencies. The implementation and saved experiment outputs provide a reproducible basis for prospective IV validation and arbitrage-constrained extensions.
