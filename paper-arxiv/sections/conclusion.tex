\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We developed a synthetic American-option pipeline that combined JumpHMM stock paths, fitted IV surfaces, contract-specific square-root IV factors, and recombining lattices. Neural and sector-specific surfaces reduced in-sample IV error relative to a global five-parameter surface, and the tested earnings inputs reduced error on one two-day temporal holdout. These results support the modular representation, while the limited splits do not establish broad temporal generalization.

The GS and LLY examples produced stock, IV, option-value, and Greek paths under an explicit 31-calendar-day and 22-transition schedule. They also clarified the source of terminal risk: IV dynamics affected interim marks and Greeks, while held-to-expiration P\&L depended on the JumpHMM terminal stock price and payoff. The open-source repository provides a basis for stronger rolling-origin validation, arbitrage-constrained surfaces, calibrated factor dynamics, and scenarios that include dividends and trading frictions.
