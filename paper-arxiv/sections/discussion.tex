\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

The main contribution is a modular framework in which the physical return generator, contract-level IV dynamics, and American-option pricer can be examined separately. The paired ablation made that separation measurable: changing only the IV module altered interim marks and liquidation risk on the same stock paths, while preserving entry marks and terminal payoffs. This establishes a practical role for the IV module in controlled scenario comparisons. The experiment also clarifies the interpretation of terminal P\&L: those outcomes depend on the selected physical return marginal and entry premium, so they cannot validate the stochastic IV factor. Applications involving early liquidation require evidence about interim marks in addition to terminal-stock behavior.

The ablation separates several sources of interim variation. Direct surface evaluation responds immediately to current moneyness and maturity. Mean reversion retains the history of earlier targets, and stochastic factors add variation around that response. Negative return coupling then redistributes exposure between puts and calls: the tested put lower tails worsened relative to the uncoupled factor, while call lower tails improved (Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:dynamic_ablation}). These changes followed imposed dynamics, not estimated risk premia or a validated trading advantage. Their size substantially exceeded the sampled lattice-depth differences. The design therefore identifies sensitivity to modeling choices; a chronological comparison with observed option marks would be needed to select among those choices on predictive grounds.

Modularity also creates a consistency obligation. A lattice can respect early exercise for one contract without ensuring that the collection of separately supplied IVs satisfies strike constraints. The GS audit found residual violations, including in the directly evaluated fitted surface, whereas LLY passed the sampled checks at the stated tolerance (Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:strike_audit}). This gives a concrete reason to consider arbitrage-constrained surface simulation~\cite{cont2023} before using the outputs to train agents that trade across contracts. The present construction does not inherit the joint pricing interpretation of a calibrated stock-volatility model. Its contribution is the ability to vary and diagnose components while preserving a chosen return generator.

The static calibration results should be interpreted at the same level of specificity. Flexible surfaces reduced pooled IV error, but the sharing hierarchy changed capacity and sample size together. The large residual errors and low shares of prices inside the bid--ask interval for several tickers limit claims about economically accurate marks (Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:price_error_summary}). A market-IV repricing reference helps locate discrepancies in the surface input, but does not establish broad pricing accuracy. Alpaca's reported IV is a Black--Scholes input, whereas our repricing check uses an American lattice, so exact recovery of every quote is not expected. Repeated chronological splits and simple last-observed-surface baselines would provide a stronger test of temporal transfer than the limited splits reported here.

The evidence remains limited by the short calibration window, two fitted scenario tickers at one expiry, and variance parameters and return coupling that were fixed rather than estimated. The ablation used three evaluation seeds and independent pilot normalization, but its surface fits included observations from the scenario period; its uncertainty estimates exclude calibration and pilot uncertainty. Necessary strike conditions were checked on a finite grid, with residual GS violations, and neither calendar nor dynamic no-arbitrage was established. Pricing omitted dividends, execution costs, margin, premium financing, hedging, and prior assignment; early liquidation used model marks. State- and mood-dependent IV mechanisms were inactive. The experiments support transparent simulation and sensitivity analysis, while prospective IV accuracy, market consistency, and trading performance remain unvalidated.
