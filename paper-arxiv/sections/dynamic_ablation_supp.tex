\section{IV Ablation and Strike Audit}\label{sec:supp_ablation}

At the primary endpoint, ten trading days after entry, we compared the P\&L from closing each short position under the five IV variants on shared stock paths (Table~\ref{tab:dynamic_ablation}). The paired standard errors apply to mean differences only, so we reported no interval for expected shortfall. The three evaluation seeds used the same fitted models and pilot constants. We reported them separately to show whether the differences and the numerical checks held across simulations (Table~\ref{tab:ablation_seeds}).

Because the tree priced each option separately, nothing forced the prices to be consistent across strikes. The strike audit therefore checked necessary conditions for that consistency at 401 tree steps (Table~\ref{tab:strike_audit}). The grid had eleven fixed strikes at the scenario expiration, evaluated at entry and on fifty stock paths after ten and twenty trading days. As the stock moved, some strikes left the moneyness range of the calibration data, so the audit included extrapolated surface values. Each variant and ticker supplied 2{,}222 bound checks, 2{,}020 adjacent-strike checks, and 1{,}818 convexity checks across calls and puts. Violation counts were identical at 201 and 401 steps (Table~\ref{tab:strike_audit_depth}). The saved output lists each test location and violation size.
