\section{IV Ablation and Strike Audit}\label{sec:supp_ablation}

At the fixed primary horizon, ten trading days after entry, we compared hypothetical short-position P\&L under the five IV variants on shared stock paths (Table~\ref{tab:dynamic_ablation}). The paired standard errors in the table apply to mean differences. For the expected-shortfall change from the uncoupled to the full factor, each of 5{,}000 bootstrap replicates resampled paired paths within each seed and pooled them across seeds. The standard deviation of these pooled changes gave the standard error. The three evaluation seeds used the same fitted models and pilot constants. We reported them separately to show whether the differences and the numerical checks held across simulations (Table~\ref{tab:ablation_seeds}).

Because the tree priced each option separately, nothing forced the prices to be consistent across strikes. The strike audit therefore checked necessary conditions for that consistency at 401 tree steps (Table~\ref{tab:strike_audit}). The grid had eleven fixed strikes at the scenario expiration, evaluated at entry and on fifty stock paths after ten and twenty trading days. As the stock moved, some strikes left the moneyness range of the calibration data, so the audit included extrapolated surface values. Each variant and ticker supplied 2{,}222 bound checks, 2{,}020 checks per adjacent-strike condition, and 1{,}818 convexity checks across calls and puts. Violation counts were identical at 201 and 401 steps (Table~\ref{tab:strike_audit_depth}). The saved output lists each test location and violation size.
