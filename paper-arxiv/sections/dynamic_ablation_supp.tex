The strike audit checked necessary conditions for consistent prices across strikes at 401 tree steps (Table~\ref{tab:strike_audit}). The grid had eleven fixed strikes at the scenario expiration, evaluated at entry and on fifty stock paths after ten and twenty trading days. As the stock moved, some strikes left the moneyness range of the calibration data, so the audit included extrapolated surface values. Each variant and ticker supplied 2{,}222 bound checks, 2{,}020 adjacent-strike checks, and 1{,}818 convexity checks across calls and puts. Violation counts were identical at 201 and 401 steps (Table~\ref{tab:strike_audit_depth}). The saved output lists each test location and violation size.
